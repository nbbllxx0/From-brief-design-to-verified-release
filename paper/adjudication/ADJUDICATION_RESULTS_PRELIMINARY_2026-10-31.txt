Brief-level findings
- b02: the brief explicitly says supports carry vertical and sideways load only. Existing admissible reading that also fixes x contradicts this, even if compliance changes little. Reconsider its counts=true status.
- b07, b10, b44–b48: ambiguities are real. Oracle responses are plausible completions but cannot be independently verified as the actual author's answers from the supplied package. Different plausible assumptions made without clarification must be distinguished from incorrect answers after clarification.
- b43: the keep-out intersects the entire specified bottom face over z=45–105. Either support the remaining surface and state that exception, or clarify/correct the brief. Support over void is not a physically well-defined bolted interface without additional modeling conventions. Case 21 is reasonable under the first interpretation.
- b49: refusal is appropriate because the moment has no reaction. The intended JSON nevertheless encodes a fully fixed edge by default, which resists rotation about z and is not a freely rotating hinge; do not use that spec as a physical representation of the stated mechanism.
- b50: refusal is appropriate because the specified load footprint lies inside the mandatory void. b08 also lacks a reaction in x for the specified force.
- b12: Liu and Tovar's paper describes corner planar joints, not full fixation in all three translations; label the fixed-corner finite-pad problem as adapted and verify the actual support coding before asserting exact restatement. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00158-014-1107-x
- b19: the 2013 GE challenge and fixed interfaces are real, but this rectangular envelope and load distribution should be called an adaptation, not a restatement. The official challenge specifies load through a 3/4 inch pin and fixed interfaces 2–5: https://blog.grabcad.com/ge-terms-of-service/
- The remainder of the benchmark source labels and all precise geometry/source claims have not been independently verified. A citation to a benchmark family is not proof of an exact restatement.
